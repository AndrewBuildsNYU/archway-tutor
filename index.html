How a session runs
  1. It opens by asking what you tried and where it stopped working.
  2. One focused question per turn. It will confirm a step; it will not take the last one.
  3. Three hints, each more concrete than the last. The worked solution is the rung after that.
See the system prompt The exact instructions sent with every turn. Nothing about this tutor is hidden from you.
